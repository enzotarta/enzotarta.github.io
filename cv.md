## Biography {#bio}

Enzo Tartaglione is a Full Professor of Deep Learning and Efficient AI at Télécom Paris, Institut Polytechnique de Paris, where he heads the Multimedia team. His research covers efficient and fair deep learning, with a focus on regularization: compression, pruning and sparsification of deep models, compact 3D scene representations with 3D Gaussian Splatting, and model debiasing.

His work has been selected for oral presentation at ICLR, AAAI, WACV, ICME and ICIP. He is Principal Investigator of an FIS2 Starting Grant, an ANR JCJC project and two Hi!Paris chairs, and leads a task in the Horizon Europe project ELIAS.

He serves as Area Chair for NeurIPS, CVPR, ICML, ICLR and ICASSP, as Associate Editor of *IEEE Transactions on Neural Networks and Learning Systems* and the *EURASIP Journal on Image and Video Processing*, and as Action Editor of *Transactions on Machine Learning Research*. He is an elected member of the IEEE Signal Processing Society Multimedia Signal Processing Technical Committee, an ELLIS member, an IEEE Senior Member and a Hi!Paris associate member. He received his Ph.D. in Physics from Politecnico di Torino in 2019 and his HDR from Institut Polytechnique de Paris in 2024.
